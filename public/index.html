How to play

First correct word wins control. Push the bomb away or risk a harder clue to heal 15 HP. The 15-second fuse never resets on a push; five pushes trigger impact. A hit costs 30 HP. If no one solves a clue, everyone loses 15 HP. Odd-player boundaries trigger a two-second word duel; no winner means 15 damage each. Last survivor wins. Play again keeps lobby points.
